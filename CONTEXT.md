# Water-carrier

A cozy game about an eldest daughter in Huế. Play moves through chapters that jump in time. Daily life and care are the center. Her teen years in play start in 1975, not 1960. Once the water run is her must, she is not in school. Player-facing text is English; words like đòn gánh, quán, and Huế stay. Hard history can sit at the edge of the frame; this is not a war game.

## Language

**Water-carrier**:
The eldest daughter. About seventeen in 1975. She leaves high school on Huỳnh Thúc Kháng in 1978, at eighteen. After that the water run is her must; she is not in school. She sews from eighteen until that era ends. Only then does the quán become her job, 1982–84, when she is about twenty-two to twenty-four. She does not marry. Later, in her twenties, she cares for her sister's children. This is the design name for her.
_Avoid_: protagonist, hero, player character, child as her age, switching to the Mother or to the water-carrier's children, wife, her own babies, Mom as her design name, sixteen in 1975, sixteen in 1960, school on a water day

**Mother**:
The water-carrier's mother (grandma in the family story). She sews. She is not a person the player controls. At dinner she fans the food so it is not too hot for the Father, while he is at the table. On a parcel day she walks with the water-carrier as company; she is not a second player. She does not walk to Phú Bình or to Phú Hòa. She does not carry the đòn gánh.
_Avoid_: second protagonist, playable mother, calling the water-carrier "Mother", the Mother on the Office road, the Mother on the water run

**Father**:
The water-carrier's father (grandpa in the family story). He is not a person the player controls. In 1975 he is captured at Thuận An while the family is trying to leave; she is in Đà Nẵng. The camps follow. Before that, in the school season, he can be at dinner. He comes back around 1988. The 1964 shift is separate: he surrenders and is threatened with being shot; she hears after, she does not see it.
_Avoid_: the water-carrier's husband (she has none), a playable camp prisoner, killing him in 1964, Father at dinner as the default after the camps start, a playable capture level

**Sister**:

**Day**:
An open stretch of play inside a chapter. In the school chapter the day is the walk to class, then evening. Before the 1975 scene the Father is at dinner. After the 1975 scene, cut to Huế class without him; a parcel day has no yoke: skip class, cook, walk with the Mother, Handoff, evening. In the quiet year (the sewing era): morning water run — walk light to Phú Bình, Fill, loaded walk home, Unload — then a five-minute walk to Đông in Phú Bình, then sewing, then evening. If a piece is finished that day, she then walks to Phú Hòa, Evaluate, and home before evening. She keeps morning water and sewing in this era. Evaluate and a parcel day are never the same day. In the quán chapter sewing is over: water, bike, shift, evening. Groceries are not a must there. A parcel day after school: morning water run, then cook, longer walk out with food, Handoff, light walk home, evening — no sewing or quán that afternoon.
