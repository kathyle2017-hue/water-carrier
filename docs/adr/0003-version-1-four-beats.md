# Version 1 is four beats, with the biography calendar

Early drafts used a **quiet year** around 1960–62, mixed sewing and café mornings, and a **twenties** / **care** chapter. Those overwrite family time.

Version 1, in order:

1. **School** until **1978** (a season; the **1975 scene** sits inside it).
2. **Quiet year** / **sewing** era (first stretch to *build*).
3. **Quán** **1982–84**, only after sewing is finished.
4. **Return** around **1988** (a scene, same size as 1975). **1984–88** is a jump, not a chapter.

The three seasons are even in length. She does not marry. **Twenties** / **Sister's children** are not version 1. **1964** is not a chapter. **Sewing** and the **quán** are separate eras, not a morning choice of cloth or café.
