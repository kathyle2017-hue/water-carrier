# Keep family spellings; do not “correct” them

Maps and Wikipedia will offer **Phú Hội**, **Ái Tử**, **chợ Đông Ba**. The game uses the words in her mouth until she says they are the same place.

- **Thong Hoi** — area near the **quán** (do not rename to Phú Hội).
- **Ái Thu** — only the name of the **Gate** (do not rename to Ái Tử; not a town map).
- **Đông** — the store in **Phú Bình** (do not default to chợ Đông Ba).
- **Phú Hòa** — only the name of the **Office** (not a ward map).
- **Đông** and **Fill** are two spots in **Phú Bình**. The house is on **Huỳnh Thúc Kháng**.
