# Build the water run first; Fill a stream in Phú Bình, not the Hương

The title verb is the **water run**. School, the **quán**, **Phú Hòa**, and **Ái Thu** wait. The first mock is: **Huỳnh Thúc Kháng** (alone, rain) → **Phú Bình** stream, **Fill** (water to the knees) → loaded walk home → **Unload**. **Đông** is the mock after that. Stand-in art is fine; a later character drawing and intro screen can replace it.

The **Hương** can sit on the map. She does not **Fill** there. Tourist Huế and the name “Perfume River” will keep suggesting a river-edge dip; the labor is a **stream** in **Phú Bình**, then heaviness on both shoulders on the road home. Empty jugs out are light; full jugs home are the walk. Fear of **glass** in the water and of **leeches** is a feeling; she still **Fills**. On the road, **glass** is visible and she steps around it.
