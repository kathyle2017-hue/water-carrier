# Play the water-carrier, not the Mother

The family story and the first pitch put the Mother at the center: sewing, a large household, a husband in the camps. The repo is named **water-carrier**. Version 1 has one playable body: the eldest daughter. The **Mother**, **Father**, and **Sister** are the **household** she returns to, not second players.

A first game cannot afford two control schemes, two art rigs, and two loops. Playing the Mother would make the **đòn gánh** a cutscene and fight the title. Playing both on the same **day** would be a third game.

**Status:** accepted

**Considered:** the Mother as protagonist; switching bodies; a shared-household co-op. Rejected for version 1.
